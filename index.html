About me

From cadet to captain — one rank at a time

A journey built one contract at a time

I'm Hieu — an ocean-going deck officer from Vietnam. I started at the very bottom as a deck cadet, sailing international routes and transiting the Suez Canal, then worked my way up to Third Officer and Second Officer.

In 2026, I entered the most important stage of my career so far: the Management Level course (STCW II/2) — the foundation for taking on the Chief Mate position properly and transparently. No shortcuts, no faked sea time.

Chief Mate is the next rank. Captain is where this road is heading — and I plan to earn it the same way: through real sea time, real competence, and the discipline to keep learning after every contract.

Alongside my life at sea, I run a channel for seafarers — where I share what I've lived, learned and paid for on board, so the next generation has an easier road than I did.

Who you get when I step on board

Experience

  • Deck cadet → Third Officer → Second Officer on ocean-going dry cargo vessels

Current focus
