About me

From cadet to officer

A journey built one contract at a time

I'm Hieu — an ocean-going deck officer from Vietnam. I started at the very bottom as a deck cadet, sailing international routes and transiting the Suez Canal, then worked my way up to Third Officer and Second Officer.

In 2026, I entered the most important stage of my career: the Management Level course (STCW II/2) — the foundation for taking on the Chief Officer position properly and transparently. No shortcuts, no faked sea time.

Alongside my life at sea, I run a channel for seafarers — where I share what I've lived, learned and paid for on board, so the next generation has an easier road than I did.

Professional Profile

Who you get when I step on board

I am a Vietnamese ocean-going deck officer, currently sailing as Second Officer on dry cargo vessels. I am completing my Management Level (STCW II/2) training in 2026, preparing for the Chief Officer position — step by step, with real sea time and real competence.

Experience

  • Deck cadet → Third Officer → Second Officer on ocean-going dry cargo vessels
  • International trading routes, incl. Suez Canal transits
  • Navigation watchkeeping, passage planning & chart correction

Current focus

  • Management Level course (STCW II/2) — expected completion Sep 2026
  • Cargo operations, stability & ballast management for bulk / general cargo

Working principles
